Three quick steps. Most people finish in 10 minutes. Once we have access, everything else is on us.

1Give us access to your Google Business Profile

  1. Go to business.google.com and open your business.
  2. Tap the three dots, then Business Profile settings, then People and access.
  3. Tap Add and enter brooke@gvlhandy.com.
  4. Set the role to Manager and send the invite.

Manager is enough. You stay the owner and can remove us any time. No profile yet, or it isn't verified? Tell us and we'll walk you through it.

2Send us the basics

Reply to the email with these, or tap the button below to start one:

  • Business name, phone, and website (if you have one)
  • Towns or counties you serve
  • Services you want to be known for (your top 3 to 5)
  • Hours
  • 5 to 10 photos of recent work (phone pictures are fine)
  • Your Facebook Page link, if you have one
Email us your info

3Send your recent customers

This is how we get you more reviews. Send a list of customers from the last 6 to 12 months with name and mobile number or email.

  • Only include people who are fine hearing from you again.
  • We send them a short, friendly message with a direct link to leave a Google review.
  • You approve the wording before the first one goes out.

A photo of your job list or a spreadsheet export works. Don't worry about formatting.

What happens next

  • Within 2 business days: we clean up your profile and send you the review message to approve.
  • Week 1: first review requests go out and your first Google post is published.
  • Every week after: new Google post, replies to new reviews, and a short monthly report.
